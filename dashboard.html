Key Business Insights

Critical Retention Problem

96.8% of customers make only ONE purchase. Only 3.2% return for a second order. This indicates a massive customer retention issue that needs immediate attention.

Delivery Delays Impact Satisfaction

Late deliveries (6.8% of orders) cause review scores to drop by 2.02 points. On-time delivery = 4.2 stars, late delivery = 2.18 stars.

Top Performing Category

Beleza/Saude (Beauty/Health) generates R$ 1.26M in revenue, followed by Relogios/Presentes (R$ 1.1M) and Esporte/Lazer (R$ 965K).

Geographic Concentration

Sao Paulo (SP) dominates with 41.8% of all orders, followed by Rio de Janeiro (12.8%) and Minas Gerais (11.6%). Top 3 states = 66.2% of business.

Payment Preferences

Credit card is the dominant payment method (73.9% of orders, R$ 12.4M revenue). Boleto (bank slip) is second at 19.6%.

Revenue Growth Trend

Revenue peaked in November 2017 at R$ 1.5M, likely due to Black Friday. Average monthly revenue: R$ 639K. Strong seasonality detected.
