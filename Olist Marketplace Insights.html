Where to look next

Five places the charts point to

These are ideas to test, based on what the numbers show. They are not proven causes.

Customers

Bring buyers back

97% never ordered again, so even a small rise in repeat buying adds real revenue. Follow-up offers and loyalty rewards are worth trying.

Delivery

Keep delivery promises

Late orders are only 8% of the total, but their ratings are far lower. Set delivery estimates the business can meet.

Products

Favor high-value categories

Watches and Gifts earns R$214 per order. Bed, Bath and Table earns R$110. Promoting larger baskets could lift revenue faster than adding orders.

Geography

Look beyond three states

São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais hold about 71% of customers. Other states are a smaller base with room to grow.

Growth

Find the next growth driver

Revenue has stayed near R$1.0M to R$1.1M a month since late 2017. Reaching new levels likely needs something beyond current momentum.
